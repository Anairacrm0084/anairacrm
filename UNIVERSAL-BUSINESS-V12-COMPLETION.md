# Anaira Universal Business V12 – Setup Completion Layer

This build extends the V11 universal setup with business-specific workflow modules for all non-hotel/non-restaurant business types.

## Included
- 31 non-hotel/non-restaurant business route families.
- Business-specific setup schema for each type.
- Existing domain-specific primary entities and fields preserved.
- Required workflow/setup modules are automatically merged into each business schema.
- Individual image fields and gallery fields remain supported.
- Universal media storage/RLS foundation remains included.
- Premium landing pages continue to consume business records, media, testimonials and offers.
- Salon keeps its dedicated setup/landing engine.

## Important
This is a setup/data/landing completion layer. It does not constitute independent production certification of every domain workflow, payment provider, payroll calculation, medical/legal compliance flow, or external integration.
