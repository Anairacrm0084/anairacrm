## UX
Use left enterprise navigation, KPI cards, charts, tables, quick actions, timelines, status pills and drill-down pages. Customer 360 is the central relationship view.

## Ownership
CRM owns customer relationship data.
PMS owns rooms/stays.
POS owns restaurant orders/billing.
Booking Engine owns reservations.
Accounting owns financial books.
Channel Manager owns OTA distribution.
Revenue Management owns pricing recommendation/policy.
